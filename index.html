A little more info

Questions & Answers

What is StepUp?

It’s the app used for step tracking, leaderboard positions, and the group chat.

How do I get the group link?

After your $2 entry arrives, Lilah sends the private invite to the contact information in your Venmo description.

Should my Venmo payment be private?

Yes! Especially if its description includes your phone number or email.

Do I need a smartwatch?

No. Your phone works, or you can use a supported smartwatch or wearable.

Can I run instead of walk?

Yes. Walking, running, and normal daily movement count when StepUp records them.

Can other people see my steps?

Yes. Group members can see relevant activity and rankings. You can leave after the winner is announced.

What if my steps stop syncing?

Test before September 20. If syncing fails during the challenge, mention it in the group chat right away.

What is in the Boo Basket?

The prize will be curated by Boo Basket professionals using the full entry pool. More people = better prizes!

Who all can participate?

Anyone! Feel free to send this website to any friends and family.
