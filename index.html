FAQ

What is StepUp?

StepUp is the app we’ll use to track everyone’s steps, leaderboard position, and group chat during the challenge.

When can I join the StepUp group?

The StepUp group link will be posted on this website on September 22. Once it is live, tap the button above to join “Boo Basket Step Challenge 🎃”.

Do I need a smartwatch?

No. Your phone can track steps too. You can also use a supported smartwatch or wearable if you prefer.

Can I run instead of walk?

Yes. Walking, running, and normal daily movement all count as long as StepUp records the steps.

Can other people see my steps?

Yes. Once you join the group, other participants can see your relevant step activity and leaderboard ranking. If you do not want to keep sharing after the challenge, you can leave the group once the winner is announced.

What if my steps are not syncing?

Use the days before September 25 to test your setup. If something stops syncing during the challenge, mention it in the StepUp group chat as soon as you notice.

What is in the Boo Basket?

The prize will be curated by Boo Basket professionals using the full entry pool. More people = better prizes!
