🔐 Privacy & what others can see

Your step activity is private by default in StepUp. Once you join the Boo Basket Step Challenge group, other members of that group will be able to see your relevant step activity and leaderboard ranking inside StepUp.

  • Before the challenge: if you join the group before September 25, other group members may be able to see your normal step activity during setup days.
  • During the challenge: your step totals and ranking will be visible to other participants through the StepUp leaderboard.
  • After the challenge: the group may continue showing ongoing step activity unless you leave it. If you do not want to keep sharing after October 25, you can leave the group once the winner is announced.
  • Location: the challenge is based on step counts, not where you walked. StepUp does not use GPS location to count your steps.
Good to know: joining the StepUp group means opting in to share your challenge-related step activity with the other participants.

❓ Quick FAQ

Can I run instead of walk?
Yep. If StepUp records the steps, they count.

Do I need an Apple Watch?
No. Your phone can track steps too, and StepUp supports multiple wearable ecosystems.

Can I join after September 19?
Plan on September 19 being the cutoff so everyone is set up before the challenge begins.

What happens if I forget my phone?
If you use a connected wearable, it may still sync those steps. What ultimately appears in StepUp is what counts.

Can other people see my steps?
Yes, once you join the StepUp group, other participants can see your relevant step activity and leaderboard ranking. You can leave the group after the challenge if you no longer want to share.
